Play the suit out: every card you choose is graded against the best play from what you have seen. Unlimited entries, no ruffs, no inference about the enemy hands; the defenders see all four hands and pick the card that hurts most. Odds are a-priori vacant-places weights. The engine searches declarer strategies, not per-branch guesses, and is exact for trick targets; for the most tricks on average it says when the line it found is not proven best. Each goal shows as proven, best found (with a bound no line beats), or interrupted (with that bound only).
