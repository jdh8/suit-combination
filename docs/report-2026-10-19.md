# Comparison report, 2026-10-19

Phase 6 of the [parity plan](parity-plan.md). Engine at `a84bd6e`; the
bench harness that measured it (with matchpoint comparison added) is in the
commit that adds this report. Raw rows: [bench-2026-10-19.tsv](bench-2026-10-19.tsv).

## What is claimed

- **Agreement:** on every published number we hold (SuitC, Bridge
  CCAnalyser, SuitPlay's line comparisons, Todd's 100% line), the engine
  gives the same value at the published precision, under the same model.
- **Proof:** every trick-target result in the corpus is proven, not a best
  found; every returned plan is replayed against every legal defence; an
  independent uncompressed oracle agrees on random combinations.
- **No speed or coverage tie with any competitor is claimed.** No competitor
  was run or timed here. Their own guides disclose pruning (SuitC) or no
  optimality guarantee (CCAnalyser); that is their claim, not our measurement.

The model is fixed throughout: pure declarer strategy against an omniscient
defence that may falsecard, 13/13 vacant places unless constrained, only this
suit's tricks count. Numbers from a different model are not comparable.

## Setup

AMD Ryzen 7 8700F (8 cores), 62 GB RAM, Fedora 44, Rust 1.99.0, release
build. Browser: Google Chrome 156 (beta) headless via Playwright, wasm built by
wasm-pack 0.15.0. One machine, one run each: spot measurements, not
percentiles across machines.

## Coverage

Any holding: at most 256 layouts after merging interchangeable cards (162
for any holding without constraints). Constraints: vacant places, known
cards, length and HCP ranges, first hand, finite entries, round limits, a
fixed first card, discards, one side's ruffs. Goals: at least N tricks; line
comparison at matchpoints or IMPs (contract, vulnerability, tricks needed),
with a mix of lines where lines beat each other in a cycle.

**Not covered:** overruffs; constraint combinations past 256 layouts are
rejected (`TooManyMissing`), not solved; expected tricks (dropped: neither
MP nor IMPs); lines treat defender cards not yet told apart alike.

## Not measured

Competitor speed, memory or coverage on this corpus; timings on other
machines or browsers; the oracle where its antichains pass its limit (46 of
1,000 random 2-8-missing combinations).
